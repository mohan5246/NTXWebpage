In short: this website collects only what you choose to send us in an enquiry. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. We use your enquiry to reply to you, and we do not sell or share it for marketing.

1. Who we are

This website, www.nithiyaatechnologies.com, is run by Nithiyaa Technologies (“NTX”, “we”, “us”), Puducherry, India. For anything about your information, contact us at hello@nithiyaatechnologies.com or on WhatsApp at +91 91502 43847.

2. What we collect

When you send an enquiry with “Send Enquiry”. The form sends us your name, the topic you choose, your business or college (if you add it), your email address and your message. The form also contains a hidden field that only automated spam tools fill in.

When you use “Send WhatsApp Enquiry”. This website sends nothing to us. It opens WhatsApp with your enquiry written in, and nothing reaches us until you press Send in WhatsApp. When you do, we receive the message together with your phone number and WhatsApp profile, as with any WhatsApp chat.

When you email, call or message us directly, we receive what you send and your contact details.

Information collected automatically. We do not run analytics or tracking on this website. Like any website, the services that deliver it receive technical data needed to send you the page, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request (see section 4).

The 60-second challenge runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and score are not sent to us or stored.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project, product demo or training.
  • To prepare estimates and proposals you ask for, and to provide the services you agree to.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.
  • To protect the website and our inbox from spam and misuse.

We use your information because you chose to send it to us for these purposes. We do not sell it, rent it or add you to marketing lists. We share it only with the services listed below, or where the law requires us to.

4. Services that handle your data

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives the details from “Send Enquiry” and forwards them to our inbox by email.
  • Our email provider stores the enquiry emails we receive and send.
  • WhatsApp, operated by Meta, carries WhatsApp enquiries under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may record visitors' IP addresses for security and to operate the service.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this website, so your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details.

These providers may process data outside India. Each handles it under its own privacy policy.

5. Cookies and storage

This website does not set cookies. It saves one small note in your browser's session storage so that the opening animation plays only once per visit. That note never leaves your device and is cleared when you close the browser tab.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and our replies for as long as we need them to respond to you, to work with you, and to keep reasonable business records. If you ask us to delete your enquiry, we will do so unless we have to keep it by law.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can also withdraw your consent to our using it. Write to hello@nithiyaatechnologies.com. We will reply within a reasonable time, and these rights apply as provided by Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you have a complaint about how we handled your information, write to the same address and we will look into it.

8. Our products

Arivinaa (arivinaa.com) is a separate website with its own terms and privacy policy, which apply when you use it.

PavAI by NTX previews on this website show demo data. Every patient name, appointment and payment in them is fictional. No real patient information is shown on, or collected by, this website.

9. Security

This website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and enquiries travel over encrypted connections to FormSubmit and to our inbox. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so please do not send passwords, payment details or medical information through the enquiry form.

10. Children

This website is meant for adults, businesses and institutions. If you are under 18 and interested in our training, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us with you.

11. Changes and contact

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. For any question about this policy, write to hello@nithiyaatechnologies.com.
