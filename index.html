A LITTLE STORY THAT INSPIRES US

Sometimes, a “failure” is simply an idea
waiting to be understood.

In 1968, a scientist at 3M was trying to create a very strong adhesive. Instead, he accidentally created one that was weak and reusable.

At first, it seemed like a failure.

For years, nobody knew what to do with it. Then another 3M employee had an idea: what if this adhesive could hold a piece of paper without damaging the page?

That simple idea eventually became the Post-it Note — something now used around the world.

“Not every failure is a dead end. Sometimes, it is an idea waiting for the right person to see its potential.”

At NTX, we believe every idea deserves that chance.
