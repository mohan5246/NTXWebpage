The note almost thrown away

In 1968 a scientist at 3M set out to make a very strong adhesive and made a weak, reusable one instead. For years nobody knew what it was for. Then a colleague wanted a bookmark that would stay put without damaging the page. The Post-it Note was born.

Not every failure is a dead end. Sometimes it is an idea waiting for the right person to see what it could be.

At NTX, we believe every idea deserves that chance.

Quick answers

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. Share what you need and we will suggest a practical approach and a written estimate before any work starts.

Can you build an appointment booking system?

Yes. PavAI by NTX already covers appointment booking and management, online consultations and digital payments for clinics, and we build booking systems for other businesses too.

Do you build AI agents?

Yes: assistants for customer questions, enquiry capture, data extraction and reports, always with a person able to step in.

Do you work with businesses outside Puducherry?

Yes. We follow a hybrid model: we work remotely with clients and collaborate in person when a project or business requirement calls for it.

Do you offer training for students?

Yes: classes, workshops, project guidance and career mentoring for students and professionals.

Who owns the code?

Ownership and usage rights for client-specific deliverables are agreed in writing for each project. NTX keeps its rights to pre-existing and independently developed reusable technology, such as frameworks, modules, components, tools, deployment scripts and general know-how, unless we expressly agree otherwise in writing.
